Strengthening the Orange-Fleshed Sweet Potato Value Chain in Machakos County

OFSP Connect is a digital marketplace platform supporting farmers, buyers, aggregation centres, and other value chain actors within Machakos County. The platform strengthens market access, aggregation, traceability, and coordination across the Orange-Fleshed Sweet Potato (OFSP) value chain.

Implemented by Concern Worldwide with support from KOICA in partnership with the County Government of Machakos.

Why OFSP Connect?

Farmers within the OFSP value chain often face challenges accessing reliable markets, coordinating aggregation, and connecting efficiently with buyers and other value chain actors. OFSP Connect was developed to support more structured, transparent, and digitally enabled market systems that strengthen coordination, improve market access, and promote sustainable agricultural commercialization within Machakos County.

Improved Market Linkages

Supporting stronger connections between farmers, buyers, and market opportunities.

Better Coordination

Enhancing aggregation, produce handling, and value chain coordination across different actors.

Transparency & Traceability

Promoting produce verification, quality assurance, and traceability within the marketplace.

Inclusive Digital Access

Supporting accessibility through web, SMS, and USSD integration for different user groups.

How OFSP Connect Works

1

Farmers List Produce

Farmers and farmer groups post available OFSP produce, including quantity, grade, location, and expected availability timelines.

2

Aggregation & Verification

Lead farmers and aggregation centres verify produce details, quality, and readiness before listings become available within the marketplace.

3

Buyers Access Listings

Buyers browse available produce, submit sourcing requests, and engage directly with suppliers and aggregation centres.

4

Coordination & Delivery

Aggregation centres, transport providers, and value chain actors coordinate produce collection, logistics, and delivery processes.

Implemented Through Partnership

OFSP Connect is implemented through collaboration between development, government, and technology partners working together to strengthen inclusive agricultural market systems and support sustainable OFSP value chain development in Machakos County.
