# User Guide: Farmer

This guide explains how to use the platform as a **Farmer**: posting produce, managing orders, booking pickups, responding to buyer requests, ordering inputs, and viewing analytics.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the platform in your browser and go to **Login**.
2. Enter your **phone** (or email) and **password**. Use the **eye icon** next to the password field to show or hide your password as you type.
3. Click **Sign in**. You are taken to **My Orders** where you can see and manage your orders.

> **Note:** Registration is currently disabled ("Coming Soon"). Your account is created by a staff admin from the **Users** page, or via bulk CSV upload by staff.

---

## 2. Main menu and navigation

After login, the sidebar shows:

| Menu item         | Path / Purpose |
|-------------------|----------------|
| **Buyer Requests**| View buyer demand (RFQs and sourcing requests) and respond with offers |
| **Inputs**        | Browse and order farm inputs (seeds, etc.) |
| **My Produce**    | Post and manage your produce listings |
| **Pickup Schedules** | View and book pickup slots at aggregation centres |
| **Orders**        | List and manage orders from buyers |
| **Analytics**     | View your sales and activity metrics |

Use the **menu icon** on small screens to open or close the sidebar.

In the header, you have two separate buttons: **Settings** (opens your profile page) and **Sign Out** (with a confirmation dialog before signing out).

## 3. Posting produce (My Produce)

**Essence:** This is how you **offer your produce to buyers** on the platform. By creating a listing, you make your crop visible to buyers so they can place orders. Approval by a lead farmer keeps quality and details consistent, and once approved your listing can receive orders straight away—including **advance orders** before the ready date.

**Flow: Create listing → Pending approval → Approved (ACTIVE) or Revision requested.**

1. Go to **My Produce** (sidebar).
2. You see tabs: **All Produce**, **Produce Listings**, and **Picked Up**. Summary cards show your total produce, total quantity, batches with batch IDs, and average price.
3. Click **Post produce** (or **Add listing**).
4. Fill in:
   - **Product type** (optional — from the commodity catalogue, e.g. Fresh Roots)
   - **Variety** (required — e.g. Kenya, SPK004, Kabode, from the catalogue)
   - **Quantity** and unit (default: kg)
   - **Quality grade** (required — A, B, or C, from the catalogue)
   - **Harvest date** (required — when the produce was or will be harvested)
   - **Price per kg** (required — in KES)
   - **County** (required — auto-filled from your profile)
   - **Aggregation centre** (required — searchable dropdown; auto-filled if you are assigned to one)
   - Optional: **Sub-county**, **ward**, **village** (read-only from your profile), **description**, **photos**
5. Submit. The listing is created with status **Pending lead approval**. You can also view the **On-Farm Sorting Guide** dialog for quality guidance.
6. A **lead farmer** (or staff) will approve or request changes. You get a notification.
7. If **approved**, the listing becomes **ACTIVE** and appears on the buyer marketplace (including for advance orders).
8. If **revision requested**, open the listing, fix the details, and resubmit for approval.

You can **edit** or **delete** your own listings from **My Produce** according to status rules. Use **filters** (search by variety/batch ID/location, variety, status) to find specific listings.

## 4. Managing orders from buyers (Orders)

**Essence:** Here you **see and manage every order** placed on your listings. You decide which orders to accept or reject, track payment and fulfilment, and follow the order through to collection or delivery.

**Flow: Buyer places order → You see it under Orders → Accept/Reject → Order moves through payment and fulfilment.**

1. Go to **Orders** in the sidebar.
2. You see all orders for **your** listings (any status: placed, accepted, paid, ready for collection, delivered, etc.).
3. For new orders (**Order placed**):
   - Open an order to see buyer, quantity, price, and delivery/collection details.
   - **Accept** or **Reject** the order. If you reject, you can add a reason.
4. After you accept:
   - Buyer may pay (escrow). When produce is ready, you (or the centre) update status (e.g. **Ready for collection**).
   - Buyer (or transport) collects or receives delivery; status moves to **Collected** / **Delivered**.
5. Use **Filters** (e.g. by status) and **Search** to find specific orders.

Orders from **advance** listings (expected ready date in the future) appear here as soon as the buyer places the order; fulfilment happens when the produce is ready.

## 5. Pickup schedules (delivering to aggregation centres)

**Essence:** This is how you **schedule and record delivery** of your produce to an aggregation centre. By booking a slot, you get a clear time and place to bring your crop, avoid overcrowding, and get your delivery recorded.

**Flow: View schedules → Book a slot → Deliver produce → Receipt.**

1. Go to **Pickup Schedules** in the sidebar.
2. You see **schedules** for aggregation centres (dates and available slots).
3. Select a schedule and **book a pickup slot** (e.g. for delivering your produce to the centre).
4. On the day, deliver produce to the centre in your slot. The centre may record **stock in** and you may get a **receipt** (view or download from the same area).
5. You can view past **pickup bookings** and receipts from this section.

---

## 6. Buyer requests (RFQs and sourcing requests)

**Essence:** This screen shows **buyer demand** — both **RFQs** (Requests for Quotation) and **Sourcing Requests** from buyers. You can respond with quotes or offers to secure sales. Use it to gauge demand, see what buyers are looking for, and compete for orders.

**Flow: Browse requests → View details → Submit quote (RFQ) or offer (sourcing request).**

1. Go to **Buyer Requests** in the sidebar.
2. You see a list of buyer requests as cards, showing: title, buyer name, location, quantity, price range, deadline, number of responses, and status badge.
3. Use **filters** to narrow results:
   - **Search** by title, buyer name, or location
   - **Request type**: All, RFQs, or Sourcing
   - **Product type** (from the commodity catalogue)
   - **Status**: All, Active, Urgent, Evaluating, Awarded, Closed
   - **Sort by**: Newest, Oldest, Deadline soonest, Largest quantity
4. Click a request to **View Details**:
   - For **RFQs**: click **Submit Quote** to respond with your price and terms (available while status is "published" and before the deadline)
   - For **Sourcing Requests**: click **Submit Offer** to respond with your offer (available while status is "open" or "urgent")
5. Track your submitted responses and any awards from this section.

---

## 7. Inputs (farm inputs)

**Essence:** This is the **inputs marketplace** where you **order seeds and other farm inputs** from registered input providers. You can compare products and prices, place orders, and track them in one place.

1. Go to **Inputs** in the sidebar.
2. Browse **inputs** (e.g. seeds, fertiliser) offered by **input providers**.
3. Add items to cart and place an **order**.
4. Track your **input orders** from the same area or the link provided after ordering.

---

## 8. Analytics

**Essence:** Analytics gives you a **clear picture of your sales and activity**: how much you've sold, which varieties and grades perform best, and how your listings and orders behave over time.

1. Go to **Analytics** in the sidebar.
2. View summaries of your **sales**, **orders**, **listings**, and **activity** (e.g. over time, by variety or grade).
3. Use filters (e.g. date range) if available to focus on a period.

---

## 9. Notifications and profile

- **Notifications** (bell icon) show approvals, new orders, revision requests, and other updates. Open them to go to the relevant screen.
- Click **Settings** in the header to open your **Profile** page, where you can:
  - **Edit personal info**: first name, last name, county, sub-county, ward, village, address, alternate phone, bio
  - **Change phone or email**: requires **OTP verification** — enter the new value, receive a verification code, enter the code to confirm the change
  - **Update payment details**: M-PESA number, bank name and account number (for receiving payments)
  - **Update password**
- Click **Sign Out** in the header to log out. A **confirmation dialog** will ask you to confirm before signing out.

---

## 10. Quick reference: key flows

| Goal | Steps |
|------|--------|
| Sell produce | My Produce → Post produce → Fill variety, quantity, grade, harvest date, price, centre → Submit → Wait for approval → Orders appear when buyers order |
| Handle an order | Orders → Open order → Accept or Reject → Fulfil when ready (e.g. ready for collection) |
| Respond to buyer demand | Buyer Requests → Browse RFQs/Sourcing → View Details → Submit Quote or Submit Offer |
| Deliver to centre | Pickup Schedules → Book slot → Deliver on day → View receipt |
| Buy inputs | Inputs → Browse → Order → Track in input orders |
| See performance | Analytics → View metrics and charts |
| Update profile | Settings → Edit personal info, phone/email (with OTP), payment details |
