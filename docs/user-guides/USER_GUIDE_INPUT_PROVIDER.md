# User Guide: Input Provider

This guide explains how to use the platform as an **Input Provider**: managing your product catalogue (inputs), orders from farmers, inventory, and customers.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the platform in your browser and go to **Login**.
2. Enter your **phone** (or email) and **password**. Use the **eye icon** next to the password field to show or hide your password as you type.
3. Click **Sign in**. You are taken to **My Dashboard** (input provider); the sidebar shows the input-provider menu.

> **Note:** Registration is currently disabled ("Coming Soon"). Your account is created by a staff admin from the **Users** page.

---

## 2. Main menu and navigation

After login, the sidebar shows:

| Menu item      | Path / Purpose |
|----------------|----------------|
| **My Dashboard** | Overview of your sales and orders |
| **My Inputs**  | Manage your input products (catalogue) |
| **Orders**     | View and manage orders from farmers |
| **Inventory**  | Manage stock levels for your inputs |
| **Customers**  | View farmers who have ordered from you |

Use the **menu icon** on small screens to open or close the sidebar.

In the header, you have two separate buttons: **Settings** (opens your profile page) and **Sign Out** (with a confirmation dialog before signing out).

---

## 3. My Inputs (product catalogue)

**Essence:** **My Inputs** is your **product catalogue**—the list of seeds, fertiliser, or other inputs you offer. What you add here appears on the **Inputs marketplace** for farmers to browse and order, so your catalogue drives your sales on the platform.

**Flow: Create input product → Set price and details → Farmers see it on the Inputs marketplace and order.**

1. Go to **My Inputs** in the sidebar.
2. You see your **list of input products** (e.g. seeds, fertiliser, tools). Each may show name, category, unit, price, and stock level.
3. **Add a new input**: click **Add product** (or **New input**). In the form, enter:
   - **Name** (e.g. "OFSP vines – Kenya variety")
   - **Category** (e.g. seeds, fertiliser, tools) if the form has it
   - **Unit** (e.g. kg, pack, litre)
   - **Price** (per unit)
   - **Description** (short text for farmers)
   - Optional: **image**, **stock quantity** (or set stock in **Inventory** later)
4. Save. The input **appears on the Inputs marketplace** for farmers to browse and order. You can **edit** (name, price, description) or **deactivate** (hide from marketplace) later from the same list.
5. Keep your catalogue up to date so farmers see accurate products and prices; update **Inventory** when you receive stock or fulfil orders.

---

## 4. Orders (from farmers)

**Essence:** **Orders** is where you **see and fulfil** every order farmers place on your inputs. Confirm, update status, and add notes so farmers can track their orders.

**Flow: Farmer places order on marketplace → Order appears in your Orders → Confirm → Update status (e.g. Shipped, Delivered).**

1. Go to **Orders** in the sidebar.
2. You see **orders** placed by farmers in a list or table. Each may show order number, customer (farmer), items, quantity, total, date, and **status** (e.g. Pending, Confirmed, Processing, Shipped, Delivered).
3. Use **status filter** (e.g. Pending, Delivered) and **search** (customer name, order number) if available to find orders.
4. Click an order to open it. You see **customer** (farmer) name and contact, **items** (product, quantity, price), **total**, and **delivery/collection** details.
5. **Confirm** the order: if the status is Pending, click **Confirm** to accept it. Optionally add **notes** (e.g. expected dispatch date).
6. **Update status** as you fulfil: e.g. **Processing** (preparing), **Shipped** (dispatched—add tracking if you have it), **Delivered** (farmer has received). Farmers may see these updates in their input orders view.
7. If you need to **cancel** or **reject** an order, use the option in the order details if available; inform the farmer if needed.

---

## 5. Inventory

**Essence:** **Inventory** is where you **manage stock levels** for each input. Update quantities when you receive stock or fulfil orders so farmers don't order out-of-stock items and you can plan restocking.

**Flow: Open Inventory → See list of products and quantities → Update quantities when you receive stock or fulfil orders.**

1. Go to **Inventory** in the sidebar.
2. You see your **products** with **current stock quantity** per item. The list may also show low-stock warnings or "out of stock" labels.
3. **Update stock**: open a product or use an "Edit quantity" or "Adjust stock" action. Set the new **quantity** (e.g. after receiving stock from your supplier or after fulfilling orders). Save.
4. When stock is **low** or **zero**, consider pausing sales for that product (e.g. deactivate in **My Inputs**) or restocking and updating **Inventory** again.

---

## 6. Customers

**Essence:** **Customers** is your **list of farmers** who have ordered from you, with profile and order history. Use it for follow-up, support, and understanding who your buyers are.

**Flow: Open Customers → See list of farmers → Open customer for profile and order history.**

1. Go to **Customers** in the sidebar.
2. You see **farmers** who have placed at least one order with you. The list may show name, phone, location, number of orders, last order date.
3. Use **search** (name, phone) if available to find a customer.
4. Click a customer to view **profile** (name, contact, location) and **order history** (list of past orders, items, totals, status). Use this for **follow-up** (e.g. repeat orders, support) and to understand demand.

---

## 7. Notifications and profile

- **Notifications** (bell icon) may alert you to **new orders**. Open them to go to **Orders** and confirm or fulfil.
- Click **Settings** in the header to open your **Profile** page, where you can update personal details, change your phone or email (with OTP verification), or update your password.
- Click **Sign Out** in the header to log out. A **confirmation dialog** will ask you to confirm before signing out.

---

## 8. Quick reference: key flows

| Goal | Steps |
|------|--------|
| Sell an input | My Inputs → Add product (name, price, unit, description) → Save → Farmers order from marketplace |
| Handle order | Orders → Open order → Confirm → Update status (Processing, Shipped, Delivered) and add notes |
| Manage stock | Inventory (or My Inputs) → Update quantity per product when you receive stock or fulfil orders |
| See who bought | Customers → View list → Open customer for profile and order history |
| Update profile | Settings → Edit personal info, phone/email (with OTP) |
