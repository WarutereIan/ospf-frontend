# User Guide: Transport Provider

This guide explains how to use the platform as a **Transport Provider**: managing pickup schedules, transport requests, collection, active and completed deliveries, and earnings.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the platform in your browser and go to **Login**.
2. Enter your **phone** (or email) and **password**.
3. Click **Sign in**. You are taken to **My Dashboard** (transport provider); the sidebar shows the transport menu.

---

## 2. Main menu and navigation

After login, the sidebar shows:

| Menu item          | Path / Purpose |
|--------------------|----------------|
| **My Dashboard**   | Overview of your deliveries and activity |
| **Pickup Schedules** | Create and manage pickup schedules (dates, slots, routes) |
| **Requests**       | View and accept/assign transport requests |
| **Collection**     | Manage collection of produce (e.g. from farmers or centres) |
| **Active Deliveries** | View deliveries in progress; update status |
| **Completed**      | View completed deliveries and history |
| **Earnings**       | View payments and earnings from deliveries |

Use the **menu icon** on small screens to open or close the sidebar.

---

## 3. Pickup Schedules

**Essence:** **Pickup Schedules** define **when and where** you collect produce from farmers (e.g. by date, centre, and locations/slots). Farmers book slots against these schedules, so you know who is delivering when and can run collection in an organised way.

**Flow: Create schedule (date, centre, route) → Add slots/locations → Farmers book slots → You use schedule for collection runs.**

1. Go to **Pickup Schedules** in the sidebar.
2. You see your **schedules** in a list (e.g. by date, aggregation centre). Each may show date, centre name, locations or slots, and number of bookings.
3. **Create** a new schedule: click **Add schedule** (or **New schedule**). Enter:
   - **Date** (when collection will happen)
   - **Aggregation centre** (where produce will be delivered or which centre this schedule serves)
   - **Pickup locations** (e.g. villages, wards, or specific points) and **time slots** if you use them (e.g. 9–11, 11–13)
4. Save. The schedule is **visible to farmers** so they can **book a pickup slot** (e.g. choose a location and time) to deliver their produce. You (or the centre) use the same schedule to plan and run the collection.
5. **Open a schedule** to see **bookings**: which farmers have booked which slot/location. Use this to plan your run, and to view or issue **receipts** for farmers who have delivered. You can **edit** (add or remove slots) or **cancel** the schedule if needed before the date.
6. On the **day of collection**, go to each location/slot, collect produce from farmers who booked, and mark collections done in **Collection** (or as your process requires). The centre may then record **stock in** from your collection.

---

## 4. Requests (transport requests)

**Essence:** **Requests** is where **delivery jobs** appear—e.g. moving orders from aggregation centre to buyer. You accept or assign them, then update status (Picked up, In transit, Delivered) so buyers and the platform can track delivery.

**Flow: Buyer or system creates transport request → Request appears in Requests → You accept/assign → Fulfil delivery → Update status to Delivered.**

1. Go to **Requests** in the sidebar (or **Transport Requests**).
2. You see **transport requests** in a list. Each may show origin (e.g. aggregation centre), destination (e.g. buyer address), order reference, quantity, preferred date/time, and status (e.g. Pending, Accepted, Picked up, In transit, Delivered).
3. **Open a request** to see full details: origin, destination, contact, order info, and any special instructions.
4. **Accept** or **assign** the request: click **Accept** to take it yourself, or **Assign** to a driver/run if you manage a team. The status may change to “Accepted” or “Assigned.”
5. **Fulfil the delivery**: go to origin, **pick up** the produce (link to order/stock out if required), then deliver to destination. As you progress, **update status**:
   - **Picked up** (when you have the produce)
   - **In transit** (on the way)
   - **Delivered** (when the buyer has received it). Add **notes** or **proof of delivery** if the form allows.
6. The **buyer** may see status updates on their **Deliveries** or **My Orders** page and can **confirm delivery** when they receive the produce. Your **Completed** and **Earnings** screens will reflect the finished delivery.

---

## 5. Collection

**Essence:** **Collection** is where you **manage collection runs**—e.g. produce to collect from farmers or ward points according to your pickup schedules. Mark collections done and link to stock in at the centre so the chain from farm to centre is recorded.

**Flow: Open Collection → See tasks by schedule/location → Run collection → Mark collections done.**

1. Go to **Collection** in the sidebar.
2. You see **collection tasks** (e.g. by pickup schedule or by location): which farmers have booked, which locations/slots to cover, and what to collect.
3. Use the list to **plan your run**: order of locations, times, and farmers. On the day, go to each location, **collect produce** from the farmers who booked, and **mark** each collection as done (e.g. “Collected” or “Received”) in the UI if the page allows.
4. If your process requires, **link to stock in** at the aggregation centre: the centre staff may record the received produce in **Stock In** using your collection run or receipts. Your **Pickup Schedules** bookings and **Collection** status help keep the chain traceable.

---

## 6. Active Deliveries and Completed

**Essence:** **Active Deliveries** shows **current runs** so you can update status and add notes or proof of delivery. **Completed** is your **history** of finished deliveries for records and any dispute resolution.

**Active Deliveries — Flow: Open Active Deliveries → See in-progress deliveries → Update status (e.g. Picked up, In transit, Delivered) and add notes.**

1. Go to **Active Deliveries** in the sidebar.
2. You see **in-progress** deliveries (e.g. status Accepted, Picked up, or In transit). Each may show order, origin, destination, and current status.
3. **Update status** as you progress: open a delivery and set **Picked up**, **In transit**, or **Delivered**. Add **notes** (e.g. ETA, delay) or **proof of delivery** (e.g. photo, signature) if the form allows. When you set **Delivered**, the delivery may move to **Completed** and the buyer can confirm receipt.

**Completed — Flow: Open Completed → View history of finished deliveries.**

1. Go to **Completed** (or **Completed Deliveries**) in the sidebar.
2. You see **history** of completed deliveries: order, date, origin, destination, status (Delivered). Use for **records**, **dispute resolution**, or **reconciliation** with **Earnings**.

---

## 7. Earnings

**Essence:** **Earnings** shows **payments or earnings** from your completed deliveries (e.g. by date or order). Use it for reconciliation and to see how much you’ve earned from transport and collection.

**Flow: Open Earnings → View payments/earnings by period or order → Use for reconciliation.**

1. Go to **Earnings** in the sidebar (may use the same screen as **Payment History**).
2. You see **payments** or **earnings** from your completed deliveries. The view may list by **date**, **order**, or **run**, with amount and status (e.g. Paid, Pending).
3. Use **date filters** if available to focus on a period. Use for **reconciliation** (e.g. with your own records or with the platform operator) and to see how much you’ve earned from transport and collection.

---

## 8. Notifications and profile

- **Notifications** (bell icon or in-app) may alert you to **new transport requests** or **schedule bookings**. Open them to go to **Requests** or **Pickup Schedules**.
- Use **Settings** or **Profile** (if available) to update your details or password.

---

## 9. Quick reference: key flows

| Goal | Steps |
|------|--------|
| Set up farmer collection | Pickup Schedules → Create schedule (date, centre, locations/slots) → Save → Farmers book → Run collection on day and mark in Collection |
| Fulfil delivery request | Requests → Open request → Accept/assign → Pick up → Update status (In transit → Delivered) and add notes |
| Run collection | Collection → See tasks by schedule → Collect produce from farmers → Mark collections done |
| Track current runs | Active Deliveries → Open delivery → Update status and notes |
| See past runs and pay | Completed → View history; Earnings → View payments by date/order |
