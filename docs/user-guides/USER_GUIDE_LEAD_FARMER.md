# User Guide: Lead Farmer

This guide explains how to use the platform as a **Lead Farmer**: you have all **Farmer** features plus the ability to **approve or reject** other farmers’ produce listings so they can go live on the marketplace.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the platform in your browser and go to **Login**.
2. Enter your **phone** (or email) and **password**.
3. Click **Sign in**. You are taken to your dashboard; the sidebar shows the same items as a farmer, plus **Approvals**.

---

## 2. Main menu and navigation

After login, the sidebar shows:

| Menu item         | Purpose |
|-------------------|--------|
| **Buyer Requests**| Same as farmer: view marketplace / buyer demand |
| **Inputs**        | Browse and order farm inputs |
| **My Produce**    | Post and manage your own produce listings |
| **Pickup Schedules** | Book and view pickup slots at aggregation centres |
| **Orders**        | Manage orders from buyers on your listings |
| **Analytics**     | View your sales and activity |
| **Approvals**     | **Lead-farmer only:** Review and approve/reject other farmers’ listings |

Use the **menu icon** on small screens to open or close the sidebar. All farmer flows (posting produce, orders, pickups, inputs, analytics) work the same as in the **[Farmer guide](USER_GUIDE_FARMER.md)**; below we focus on **Approvals**.

---

## 3. Approving or rejecting listings (Approvals)

**Essence:** As a lead farmer you **quality-check** other farmers’ produce listings before they go live. By approving only listings that meet variety, grade, location, and readiness details, you keep the marketplace reliable for buyers and help farmers present their produce correctly. Rejecting with a clear reason guides farmers to fix and resubmit, so the whole chain stays consistent.

**Flow: Farmer posts listing (Pending lead approval) → You review in Approvals → Approve (ACTIVE) or Reject (Revision requested).**

1. Go to **Approvals** in the sidebar (path: `/dashboard/lead-farmer`).
2. You see a list of **pending** produce listings (status: **Pending lead approval**) from other farmers. If the list is empty, no listings are waiting for your review.
3. Optional: use **filters** (e.g. county, ward, aggregation centre) if the page supports them to focus on your area.
4. Click a listing to open it and review:
   - **Variety** (e.g. Kenya, SPK004, Kabode)
   - **Quantity** and unit (kg)
   - **Quality grade** (A, B, or C)
   - **Expected ready date** (when produce will be at aggregation centre)
   - **Location**: county, sub-county, ward, village
   - Optional: price per kg, aggregation centre, images
5. Decide:
   - **Approve**: Click **Approve**. The listing becomes **ACTIVE** and appears on the buyer marketplace. Buyers can place orders (including advance orders if the ready date is in the future). The listing disappears from your pending list and the farmer is notified.
   - **Reject**: Click **Reject** and enter a **reason** (e.g. “Grade does not match description”, “Expected date too vague”). The listing goes to **Revision requested**. The farmer is notified and can edit the listing and resubmit for approval. The listing disappears from your pending list.
6. After you approve or reject, you can move to the next pending listing or leave the page.

You cannot approve or reject your **own** listings; they appear in the queue for another lead farmer or staff to review.

---

## 4. Your own listings and orders (as a farmer)

**Essence:** Your own **My Produce** and **Orders** work like any farmer’s: you post listings (which another lead farmer or staff approves), accept or reject buyer orders, and use Pickup Schedules. Lead farmer is an extra responsibility on top of being a farmer—your selling and ordering flows are unchanged.

1. Your **own** listings: when you post from **My Produce**, they are created with status **Pending lead approval** and appear in the **Approvals** queue for **another** lead farmer (or staff) to approve. You do not see your own listings in your Approvals list. Unless your role is configured to self-approve, someone else must approve them.
2. **Orders**: go to **Orders** in the sidebar to see and manage orders placed on **your** listings. Accept or reject, track payment, and follow through to collection or delivery exactly as in the Farmer guide.
3. **Pickup Schedules**, **Inputs**, **Analytics**, **Buyer Requests**: use these exactly as described in the **[User Guide: Farmer](USER_GUIDE_FARMER.md)** (sections 5–8).

---

## 5. Notifications and profile

- **Notifications** (bell icon or in-app) can alert you when new listings are **pending approval**, or when your own listings are approved or revision-requested. Open a notification to go to the relevant screen.
- Use **Settings** or **Profile** (if available in the header) to update your details, location, or password.

---

## 6. Quick reference: key flows

| Goal | Steps |
|------|--------|
| Review pending listings | Approvals → Open listing → Review details → Approve or Reject (with reason) |
| Post your own produce | My Produce → Add listing → Submit → Wait for another lead farmer/staff to approve |
| Handle orders on your listings | Orders → Open order → Accept or Reject → Fulfil when ready (same as farmer) |
| Book pickup / buy inputs / view analytics | Follow **[User Guide: Farmer](USER_GUIDE_FARMER.md)** sections 5, 7, 8 |
