# Stakeholder Input Request - Executive Summary

## Purpose
As we develop the OFSP Marketplace Platform, we need detailed input from stakeholders who understand the current value chain operations. This information will ensure the platform accurately reflects real-world processes and addresses actual user needs.

## Critical Information Gaps

### 1. **User Ecosystem** (Priority: HIGH)
We need detailed profiles of all user types: farmers, buyers, input providers, transport providers, county staff, Concern staff, and aggregation centre officers. Specifically:
- Current workflows and pain points
- Technology literacy and access
- Communication preferences
- Success metrics and expectations

**Why it matters**: Ensures the platform serves real needs and is accessible to all users.

### 2. **Aggregation Centre Operations** (Priority: URGENT)
We need step-by-step workflows for:
- Produce receiving and quality inspection
- Storage and inventory management  
- Order fulfillment and dispatch
- Documentation and record-keeping

Also: organizational structure, facility capacity, equipment available.

**Why it matters**: The system must accurately model centre operations to be useful for daily management.

### 3. **Quality Assessment System** (Priority: URGENT)
We need clarity on:
- Specific metrics for OFSP quality (size, color, texture, variety)
- Current grading standards and categories
- How quality is assessed and recorded
- Variety-specific attributes and preferences

**Why it matters**: Quality assessment is central to fair pricing and market access.

### 4. **Product Tagging & Traceability** (Priority: HIGH)
Key decisions needed:
- Should produce be tagged at farm or aggregation centre?
- What information should QR codes contain?
- How will tags work with different packaging types?
- Who scans codes and what devices do they use?

**Why it matters**: Traceability system design affects packaging, operations, and buyer confidence.

### 5. **Reporting Requirements** (Priority: MEDIUM)
Beyond standard reports (sales, inventory, quality trends), we need to know:
- What specific reports do county officers need?
- What M&E indicators does Concern track?
- What format and frequency is most useful?
- What data visualization preferences exist?

**Why it matters**: Reports must align with existing workflows and decision-making processes.

### 6. **Current Marketplace Processes** (Priority: HIGH)
Understanding today's reality:
- How price discovery and negotiation works
- How orders are placed and confirmed
- Payment systems and timing
- Transportation arrangements
- Information flow between parties
- Trust-building mechanisms

**Why it matters**: The platform must improve, not disrupt, working processes. Understanding current operations helps identify what to digitize and what to preserve.

## Additional Context Needed

### Current Challenges
- What inefficiencies exist in current processes?
- What information gaps cause problems?
- What disputes commonly arise?
- What seasonal challenges occur?

### Technology Context  
- Mobile network coverage in production areas
- Device access (smartphones vs feature phones)
- Electricity reliability at centres
- Current digital tools in use

### Sustainability
- How will the platform be sustained long-term?
- What partnerships are needed?
- How can it scale to other counties/crops?

## Why Your Input Matters

**For Farmers**: Ensures the platform addresses your real needs, improves your market access, and is easy to use.

**For Centre Staff**: Makes daily operations easier, not harder. Reduces paperwork and manual tracking.

**For Buyers**: Improves product availability visibility, quality assurance, and transaction efficiency.

**For County Officers**: Provides data needed for planning, policy, and reporting, without extra burden.

**For Concern Staff**: Captures impact data, supports M&E, and demonstrates project value.

**For All**: A platform that reflects real needs, not assumptions, is more likely to be adopted and sustained.
