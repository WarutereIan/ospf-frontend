# Stakeholder Information Requirements for OFSP Marketplace Development

## Document Purpose

As we progress in developing the Orange-Fleshed Sweet Potato (OFSP) marketplace platform, we have identified critical information gaps that require input from stakeholders with on-the-ground experience. This document outlines the specific information needed to ensure the platform accurately reflects current processes, addresses real user needs, and supports the agricultural value chain effectively.

## 1. User Ecosystem Mapping

### Current Understanding
We have identified the following user categories in the OFSP value chain:
- OFSP Farmers (smallholder and commercial)
- Input Providers (vines, fertilizers, training materials)V
- Buyers (individual consumers, retailers, wholesalers, institutional buyers, processors)
- Transport Providers (logistics coordinators, vehicle owners)
- County Government Staff (agricultural officers, extension workers)
- Concern Worldwide Project Staff (program officers, M&E specialists)
- Aggregation Centre Officers (managers, quality inspectors, warehouse staff)

### Information Needed

**1.1 Detailed User Profiles**
For each user category, we need to understand:
- **Current Role in the Marketplace**: What do they do today? How do they currently operate?
- **Pain Points**: What challenges do they face in their daily operations?
- **Workflows**: What is their typical day-to-day process? (e.g., How does a farmer currently sell produce? How does a buyer currently source OFSP?)
- **Information Needs**: What information do they need to make decisions? (pricing, availability, quality standards, etc.)
- **Communication Preferences**: How do they currently communicate with others in the value chain? (phone calls, SMS, WhatsApp, physical visits)
- **Technology Literacy**: What is their level of comfort with digital tools? Do they have smartphones? Internet access?
- **Success Metrics**: What would success look like for them in this marketplace? What outcomes would they consider valuable?

**1.2 Sub-category Differentiation**
- **Farmers**: Small-scale vs. commercial; organized groups vs. individuals; new growers vs. experienced
- **Buyers**: Scale of operations (individual, retail, wholesale, institutional); frequency of purchase; volume requirements
- **Transport Providers**: Vehicle types (motorcycle, pickup, truck); coverage area; capacity; refrigeration capabilities
- **County Staff**: Different roles and responsibilities; reporting structures; decision-making authority
- **Concern Staff**: Program focus areas; data collection needs; intervention types

## 2. Aggregation Centre Operations

### Current Understanding
Aggregation centres serve as critical nodes in the value chain, facilitating the collection, quality assessment, storage, and distribution of OFSP produce.

### Information Needed

**2.1 Organizational Structure**
- **Hierarchy**: What is the organizational structure of aggregation centres?
  - Are there multiple centres in a county/region?
  - Is there a hub-and-spoke model (main centres and satellite collection points)?
  - Who reports to whom? What are the decision-making levels?
  - How are centres managed (Concern-managed, cooperative-run, county-government, private)?

**2.2 Physical Infrastructure**
- What storage facilities exist? (cold storage, ambient, packaging areas)
- What is the typical capacity of a centre? (square meters, tonnage)
- What equipment is available? (weighing scales, washing facilities, sorting tables, packaging equipment)
- What are the power and connectivity situations? (grid power, generator, solar; mobile network, internet)

**2.3 Produce Processing Workflow**
We need a detailed, step-by-step workflow for:

**Inbound Process (Farmer to Centre)**
1. How is produce delivered? (farmer brings, centre collects, third-party transport)
2. What documentation is required at delivery? (paper forms, digital records, farmer ID)
3. Who conducts initial inspection? (officer role, qualifications)
4. What happens during inspection? (sampling, weighing, grading)
5. How are quality assessments recorded? (paper, digital, both)
6. What feedback is given to the farmer? (verbal, written, digital notification)
7. How is payment processed? (immediate, scheduled, mobile money, bank transfer)
8. What happens to rejected produce? (return to farmer, disposal, alternative market)

**Storage and Handling**
1. How is produce stored? (packaging type, stacking method, temperature control)
2. How long is typical storage duration? (hours, days, weeks)
3. What quality checks are performed during storage? (visual inspection, condition monitoring)
4. How is inventory tracked? (manual logs, digital systems, batch numbering)
5. What happens when quality deteriorates? (markdown, waste disposal, processing)
6. How is first-in-first-out (FIFO) managed?

**Outbound Process (Centre to Buyer)**
1. How are buyer orders received? (phone, written, digital platform)
2. How is produce selected for orders? (quality matching, batch selection)
3. What packaging is used for different buyer types? (crates, bags, specialized packaging)
4. How are orders prepared? (washing, sorting, weighing, packaging)
5. What documentation is generated? (delivery notes, invoices, quality certificates)
6. How is delivery coordinated? (buyer collects, centre delivers, third-party logistics)
7. What happens if the buyer rejects produce? (return, credit, dispute resolution)

## 3. Quality Assessment and Grading System

### Current Understanding
OFSP has unique quality characteristics that affect market value and buyer preferences.

### Information Needed

**3.1 Quality Metrics**
What specific metrics are currently used or should be used to assess OFSP quality?

**Physical Characteristics**
- **Size/Weight**: What are the grade categories? (e.g., Small: 100-200g, Medium: 200-400g, Large: 400g+)
- **Shape**: Does shape matter? (uniform, curved, misshapen)
- **Skin Condition**: What surface defects are assessed? (blemishes, cuts, disease marks)
- **Color**: How is skin color evaluated? (visual comparison, color charts, digital tools)

**Internal Quality**
- **Flesh Color**: How is the orange intensity measured? (visual scale, beta-carotene testing)
- **Texture**: What textural attributes matter? (firmness, moisture content, fiber)
- **Disease/Damage**: What internal defects are checked? (rot, pest damage, hollow heart)

**Variety-Specific Attributes**
- **Variety Identification**: How are different OFSP varieties identified? (by vine characteristics, tuber appearance, farmer declaration)
- **Variety Preferences**: Do different buyers prefer different varieties? Why?
- **Traceability to Vine**: Is it important to track which vine produced the tubers? How is this currently done?

**3.2 Grading Standards**
- What grading system is currently used? (Grade A/B/C, numerical scores, pass/fail)
- Are there different standards for different markets? (export, institutional, retail, processing)
- How consistently are standards applied across centres?
- What training do quality inspectors receive?

## 4. Product Tagging and Traceability

### Current Understanding
Traceability is important for quality assurance, food safety, and market access, particularly for institutional and export markets.

### Information Needed

**4.1 Current Tagging Practices**
- How is produce currently identified/tracked? (batch numbers, farmer tags, nothing)
- What information is recorded about each batch? (source, date, variety, weight, quality)
- How is this information communicated along the value chain?

**4.2 Proposed QR Code System**
We envision a QR code-based traceability system, but need clarity on:

**Tagging Point**
- Should produce be tagged at the farm level (pre-delivery)?
- Should tagging occur at the aggregation centre (upon receipt)?
- Should there be dual tagging (farmer pre-tag, centre verification tag)?

**Packaging and Tags**
- What type of packaging is being used? (individual bags, crates, bulk bins)
- Are there specialized OFSP bags being introduced? (branded, with built-in QR)
- Should tags be on packaging or individual tubers? (considering handling and sorting)
- Are reusable crates/containers in use? (requiring tag management)

**Information to Encode**
What information should the QR code link to?
- **Basic Identification**: Batch ID, centre ID, receipt number
- **Source Information**: Farmer ID, farm location, harvest date, variety
- **Quality History**: Initial grade, quality check dates and results, storage duration
- **Handling History**: Dates of each transfer, storage conditions, temperature logs
- **Verification Status**: Authenticity confirmation, certification status

**Verification Process**
- Who scans the QR codes? (centre staff, buyers, transport, end consumers)
- What device do they use? (smartphones, dedicated scanners)
- What happens if a tag is damaged or unreadable?
- How is the QR system maintained if packaging changes hands?

**4.3 Impact on Operations**
- How would QR tagging affect packaging choices? (cost, durability, visibility)
- What training would staff need?
- What happens with mixed batches or repackaging?
- How would this system work in areas with poor connectivity?

## 5. Reporting and Analytics

### Current Understanding
Data-driven decision-making requires appropriate reports and analytics tailored to different user needs.

### Information Needed

**5.1 Current Reporting Practices**
- What reports are currently generated? (frequency, format, recipients)
- How is data currently collected? (paper forms, spreadsheets, existing systems)
- What decisions are informed by these reports?
- What challenges exist with current reporting? (time lag, accuracy, accessibility)

**5.2 Standard Reports (Anticipated)**
We plan to provide:
- **Sales Reports**: Transaction volumes, values, trends by time period
- **Production Volume Reports**: Supply from farmers, by location/variety/time
- **Quality Trend Reports**: Acceptance rates, common defects, seasonal patterns
- **Demand Forecasting**: Predicted demand based on historical data and trends
- **Inventory Reports**: Stock levels, aging analysis, wastage rates
- **Farmer Performance**: Volumes, quality, reliability, growth over time
- **Price Analytics**: Price trends, price comparisons, market dynamics

**5.3 Specialized Reports Needed**
What additional reports would be valuable?

**For County Agricultural Officers**
- What specific metrics do you report to the county government?
- What information helps you plan extension services?
- What data is needed for policy decisions?

**For Concern Project Staff**
- What M&E indicators are tracked?
- What donor reporting requirements exist?
- What information helps assess project impact?
- What data is needed for adaptive management?

**For Aggregation Centre Managers**
- What operational reports help daily management?
- What financial reports are needed?
- What information helps optimize logistics?

**For Farmers**
- What information would help them improve production?
- What market information influences their decisions?
- How should information be presented? (SMS summaries, visual dashboards, printed reports)

**5.4 Report Format Preferences**
- **Digital Dashboards**: Web-based, mobile app, both?
- **Downloadable Files**: PDF, Excel, CSV, Word?
- **Scheduled Reports**: Email delivery, SMS alerts, app notifications?
- **Custom Reports**: Ability to filter and customize report parameters?
- **Data Visualization**: Charts, graphs, maps, tables - which are most useful?

## 6. Current Marketplace Processes and Challenges

### Information Needed

**6.1 Price Discovery and Negotiation**
- How are prices currently determined? (fixed, negotiated, market-based)
- Who sets prices? (farmers, centres, buyers, government)
- How do farmers know what price to expect?
- How much price negotiation occurs? Where in the process?
- Are there reference prices or price bulletins?

**6.2 Order and Transaction Process**
- How do buyers currently place orders? (phone calls, SMS, physical visit)
- How far in advance are orders placed? (same day, days, weeks)
- How are orders confirmed? (verbal, written, deposit)
- What happens when supply cannot meet demand?
- What happens when a buyer cancels an order?
- How are disputes resolved? (complaints about quality, quantity, payment)

**6.3 Payment Systems**
- How are farmers currently paid? (cash, mobile money, bank transfer, vouchers)
- What is the typical payment timing? (immediate, daily, weekly, monthly)
- Who manages payment flows? (centre staff, financial institutions, mobile money agents)
- What payment challenges exist? (delays, transaction costs, trust issues)
- Are there any credit or advance payment systems?

**6.4 Logistics and Transportation**
- How is transportation currently arranged? (farmer responsibility, centre arranges, buyer arranges)
- What vehicles are commonly used? (motorcycle, pickup, truck)
- How are transport costs determined and allocated?
- Are there cold chain requirements? How are they met?
- What challenges exist with current transport? (cost, reliability, quality preservation)

**6.5 Information Flow**
- How do farmers learn about market demand?
- How do buyers learn about product availability?
- How is information about quality standards communicated?
- What information gaps cause inefficiencies?
- How would improved information flow help the value chain?

**6.6 Seasonality and Supply Management**
- What are the production seasons? (peak harvest, lean periods)
- How is supply managed during peak periods? (storage, processing, multiple buyers)
- How are lean periods addressed? (stored produce, alternative crops, variety staggering)
- How do prices fluctuate seasonally?

**6.7 Quality and Food Safety**
- What food safety practices are currently in place? (washing, handling, storage)
- Are there any certifications or standards required? (organic, GlobalGAP, national standards)
- How is contamination prevented? (packaging, handling, storage)
- What happens when quality issues are identified post-delivery?

**6.8 Trust and Relationships**
- How important are personal relationships in current transactions?
- What mechanisms build trust between parties? (reputation, guarantees, intermediaries)
- What are common sources of mistrust or conflict?
- How would a digital platform need to support trust-building?

**6.9 Market Linkages**
- How do farmers currently access different market segments? (direct, through centres, through traders)
- What barriers prevent farmers from accessing higher-value markets?
- How do institutional buyers (schools, hospitals) currently procure OFSP?
- What role do middlemen/traders play? (positive, negative, necessary)

**6.10 Training and Capacity Building**
- What training do farmers currently receive? (agronomic, post-harvest, business skills)
- What training do centre staff receive? (quality assessment, record-keeping, customer service)
- What knowledge gaps exist?
- How is training currently delivered? (field days, workshops, extension visits, digital content)
- How would the platform support ongoing learning?

## 7. Additional Technical Considerations

### Information Needed

**7.1 Connectivity and Infrastructure**
- What is the mobile network coverage in production areas? (2G, 3G, 4G, gaps)
- What is internet penetration among users?
- What proportion of users have smartphones vs. feature phones?
- What is the reliability of electricity supply at centres?
- What backup systems are in place? (generators, solar, battery packs)

**7.2 Data Privacy and Security**
- What data privacy concerns exist? (farmer data, price information, buyer details)
- What regulatory requirements apply? (data protection laws, agricultural data governance)
- What security measures are expected? (user authentication, data encryption, audit trails)
- Who should have access to what data? (role-based access control)

**7.3 Integration with Existing Systems**
- What digital tools are currently in use? (M&E systems, financial systems, communication platforms)
- What data needs to be shared between systems? (farmer registries, transaction data, impact metrics)
- What integration challenges should we anticipate?
- What data formats are standard? (Excel, CSV, APIs, databases)

**7.4 Sustainability and Scalability**
- How will the platform be sustained after project funding? (user fees, transaction fees, government support)
- What plans exist for scaling to other counties or crops?
- What features should be designed with scalability in mind?
- What partnerships are needed for long-term success? (telcos, financial institutions, government)
