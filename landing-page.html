The Operating System for OFSP

From planting material to final processing, we digitize every step of the Orange-Fleshed Sweet Potato value chain.

Marketplace & RFQs

A unified hub for Buyers to post RFQs and Sourcing Requests. Farmers can browse opportunities, submit quotes, and negotiate terms directly via the platform.

Secure Escrow Payments

Funds are held securely in escrow. Payments are released to farmers and transporters only after digital receipt confirmation and delivery acceptance.

Quality & Traceability

Batch-level tracking with QR codes. Aggregation Managers record stock-in and perform standardized quality checks (size, color, dry matter).

Logistics & Input Markets

Transport Providers publish pickup schedules on routes. Farmers book slots. Input Providers list high-quality vines and fertilizers for purchase.
